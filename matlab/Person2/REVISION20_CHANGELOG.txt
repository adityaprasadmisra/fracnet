REVISION 20 CHANGELOG
=====================

Only functional estimator change:
- Every valid raw Nelder-Mead endpoint that beats the current global best
  ML objective is now analytically LM-polished.
- Previously, polishing also required the endpoint to improve its own
  seed by a fixed threshold. This could leave a globally winning endpoint
  unpolished even though the analytic LM solver could potentially improve it.
- The LM result is retained only if its valid unregularized ML objective
  is strictly lower than the raw endpoint.

Preserved:
- Person 1 TX/RX files are not included or modified.
- apply_fractional_doppler_channel.m unchanged from Revision 19.
- fractional_doppler_dictionary.m unchanged from Revision 19.
- Same channel model, dictionary grid, training setup, objective,
  condition gates, final gain ridge, and all search/refinement budgets.
- Evaluator and test-driver logic unchanged apart from revision labels.
- Installation checker renamed to check_revision20_installation.

No MATLAB runtime was available. Performance claims require the user's
same-seed Revision 19 vs Revision 20 run.
